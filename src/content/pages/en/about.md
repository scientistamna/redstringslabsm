**Every teen deserves a head start. Not just the ones who can pay for one.**

I studied data science at master's level. Then I spent months going through courses, websites and YouTube channels, and I noticed that much of what I found was already out of date. Technology and AI change faster than any curriculum can keep up with.

That matters for teens. They are growing up in a world that runs on data and AI, while much of what they are taught was built for a world that has already moved on. The teens who have resources, tutors and the latest tools can pull ahead early. Everyone else is left to catch up alone.

## Two things that do not change

Underneath all the changing tools, two things stay the same: **data** and **code**. Learn to question data, and learn to write the code that works with it, and you can learn any new tool that comes along. So I built Red Strings Lab around them.

## How it works

- **A new case every Tuesday and Friday.** Each case is a short investigation: ask a real question, collect evidence, and catch a real trick, like a chart that lies.
- **Free, and in your browser.** The site needs no account, and you do not need to code to start. Some of the tools we use have their own sign-in or age rules. We always say so, and we give a free alternative.
- **Four ranks.** Rookie (data basics), Analyst (AI without code), Inspector (Python) and Chief (real projects of your own).

## What we promise

- **We tell you what is made up.** Many cases use made-up data so we know the truth and can show you where the trick is. We always say so. When we use real data, we credit the source.
- **We collect no personal data from teens.** No names, no faces, nothing sensitive. Parents or other adults handle any email signup.
- **We check our tools.** Each tool lists its age notes and a free alternative, and the date we last checked its terms.
- **We would rather be honest than impressive.** A careful result that ends open beats a dramatic one that hides its flaws.
- **We are independent.** Red Strings Lab is not affiliated with Google, Scratch, MIT, Gapminder or any tool or company we show.

Every dataset is hiding something. Let's teach teens to find it.
