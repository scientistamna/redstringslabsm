Each tool lists what it is for, any account or age rules, and a free alternative. Tool rules change, so check each tool's own terms and age rules before you sign up. We update this page as we check each tool.

## Google Forms and Google Sheets

- **Used for:** Cases 1, 2, 4, 5, 6 and 7. Collecting survey answers, cleaning data and making charts.
- **Account notes:** needs a Google account. Age rules apply, so younger teens may need an adult's or a school account.
- **Free alternative:** any form tool plus LibreOffice Calc.

## LibreOffice Calc

- **Used for:** the free alternative to Google Sheets in every spreadsheet case.
- **Account notes:** a free, open-source desktop app. No account or sign-in.

## Gapminder Tools

- **Used for:** Case 3, exploring income and happiness across countries.
- **Account notes:** free, runs in the browser, no sign-up. Charts are credited to Gapminder.org under a CC-BY license.
- **Free alternative:** another open data chart tool whose license you have checked.
