title: Parents and teachers
description: What Red String Lab teaches, and how we keep it safe for teens.
---
Red String Lab teaches data and AI literacy to ages 13 and up. Every case is a short investigation that ends with something the student built.

## Safety rules we follow

- Each case lists its tools, which ones need an adult's account, and a free alternative.
- Real data stays anonymous. We ask for no names, no emails and no school names.
- We show no faces and no real names, and we publish nothing made by a young person without written parent consent.
- Email signups are for parents or other adults only. We do not collect personal data from teens.

## What adult help is needed

Mostly none. Some tools need an account, and we say so at the top of each case.
