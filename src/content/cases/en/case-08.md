Seven cases: averages that lie, charts that lie, links that are not links, samples that cheat, blanks that hide, and a file full of mess. Now you stop watching and do it yourself.

You pick a question, collect real data, check it the way we have learned, and write a one-page case file. The tool is a template. The standard is honesty, not a perfect answer.

## Step 1: a good question

A good question has a number for an answer: how many hours, how many times, how many. Ask at least ten people. Rules before you start:

- No names.
- Ask permission, and let anyone say no.
- Skip anything sensitive, like health, family or anything that could embarrass someone.
- If a question needs a name to answer, change the question.

## Step 2: the example

The kit includes a finished example built from the made-up Case 1 data.

- **Question:** do people who sleep less use their phones more?
- **Who was asked:** ten friends, easy to ask, which is a biased sample, and the file says so.
- **Numbers:** average screen time 5.25, median 4.5, weirdest number 14 hours (kept and flagged), correlation -0.37.
- **Three questions:** a third thing, like stress? Yes. Reversed? Possibly. Chance? One pair on ten rows, so chance is a real risk.
- **Honest sentence:** among these ten friends, people who slept less tended to use their phones more. It cannot prove that screens cause bad sleep, or that this holds for anyone outside the friends.
- **Case status:** open. A good case file can end open.

## Step 3: the checklist

Nine checks from the last seven cases. Say Yes only if you really did it:

1. My data is anonymous.
2. I checked the average and the median, and explained my weirdest number.
3. My charts start where they should and show the whole picture.
4. If I used outside data, I credited it and checked its terms.
5. I asked the three questions: third thing, reversed, chance.
6. I wrote down who I asked, how I chose them, and who I did not ask.
7. I counted the blanks and worked out my response rate.
8. I kept the original data untouched and logged every cleaning change.
9. I wrote one honest sentence.

Nine Yeses and the sheet says Rookie: case closed.
