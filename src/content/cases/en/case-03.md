## The twist: averages hide people

Each dot is a country's average. Inside any country some people have far more than others, and the average hides that. So this chart cannot say whether more money makes any person happier. It can only compare countries.

## Case status

Money and happiness move together in this chart. Whether one causes the other, this data cannot say. Case not closed. Case opened.
