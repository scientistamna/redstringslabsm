Ten people answered a survey about sleep and screen time. The average says one thing. The middle person says another. Your job is to find out which one is telling the truth.

The data below is made up for the video. Yours will be real.

## Your mission

1. Ask at least 10 real people. Ask permission first, and a no is a no.
2. Collect no names, emails or school names. Less data means less to leak.
3. Find the average and the median of screen time. Work out why they differ.
4. Find your weirdest number, the outlier. Don't delete it silently. Say what you did and why.
5. Write one honest sentence: what do your answers show, and what do they not prove?
